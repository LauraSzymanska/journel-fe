Day 1 in Rome - di Trevi

During our trip to Rome, we had an amazing time visiting the iconic Trevi Fountain. The early morning atmosphere was magical, and we could feel the charm of the ancient city all around us. Before heading to the fountain, we stopped by a nearby bakery for breakfast, savoring fresh pastries that were simply delicious.

While capturing some photos at the fountain, we had a bit of an adventure — our camera nearly slipped out of our hands and almost fell into the water! Thankfully, we managed to catch it in time, but it added a bit of excitement to our morning. Visiting the Trevi Fountain was definitely a highlight of our Roman adventure.
